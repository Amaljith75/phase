# Phase — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Phase is a music remixing app. It is designed to work entirely on your
device.

## What Phase does with your data

- **Your music stays on your phone.** Songs you import are copied into the
  app's private storage. AI stem separation runs on your phone; your audio is
  not uploaded anywhere.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** Phase does not collect
  personal information, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash
  reports, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
- **Exports** you create are stored in the app and are only shared or saved
  elsewhere when you explicitly tap *Share* or *Save to Music*.

## Optional features that use the internet

- **Online lyrics lookup.** Only when you tap *Find online* on the lyrics
  screen, Phase sends the song's title, artist, album and duration to
  [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) to search for lyrics. No audio and no
  identifiers are sent. You can disable this in *Settings → Lyrics*.
- **Self-hosted separation server (advanced, off by default).** If you
  switch *Settings → AI separation → Engine* to *My own server*, the songs
  you separate are uploaded to the server address **you** enter. That server
  is operated by you, not by the developer.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Show progress while separating, and playback controls |
| Foreground service (media processing / data sync / media playback) | Keep separation and playback running when the app is in the background |
| Internet | The optional features above |

Phase does not request access to your contacts, location, camera,
microphone or shared storage. Files are imported through the system file
picker, which only grants access to the files you choose.

## Deleting your data

Delete a track in the Library to remove its copy, stems and projects, or
uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Children

Phase is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from anyone.
