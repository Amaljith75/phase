**App name** (max 30): `Phase – Stem Remixer`

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Phase turns your phone into a music remixing instrument.

Import any song from your phone and Phase's on-device AI splits it into four
stems — vocals, drums, bass and everything else. Then play it like an
instrument: swipe a stem up or down to change its volume, swipe right to
isolate it, swipe left to mute it.

• AI STEM SEPARATION ON YOUR PHONE
Powered by Hybrid Transformer Demucs, one of the best open music separation
models. Runs entirely on your device — no upload, no account, works offline.
Separated songs are saved, so they open instantly next time.

• A TACTILE INSTRUMENT
Four large stem pads with LED volume ladders and haptic detents. Double-tap
to mute, long-press for effects. One tap for Acapella, Instrumental (karaoke)
or Rhythm-only modes. Gestures are customisable.

• STUDIO EFFECTS, PER STEM
Reverb, delay, ping-pong echo, low- and high-pass filters, distortion,
chorus, pitch shift, 3-band EQ and pan on every stem and on the master — all
in real time with animated knobs.

• PLAY WITH TIME
Speed 0.5×–2× with optional pitch lock, reverse playback, A/B looping,
waveform scrubbing, BPM detection and tap tempo.

• SYNCED LYRICS
Line-by-line highlighted lyrics with auto-scroll. Import .lrc files, look
lyrics up online, edit them, or tap along to time-sync your own.

• RECORD & EXPORT
Record your live performance or export the full remix as WAV or MP3 —
rendered with exactly the effects you hear. Save projects and mix presets,
share your remix anywhere.

Supports MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A/AAC and OGG.
Low-latency native audio engine. No ads. No tracking.

Separation takes a few minutes per song on a recent phone and continues in
the background. Requires 4 GB RAM or more (6 GB+ recommended).
```

**Category**: Music & Audio · **Tags**: music editor, DJ, karaoke, remix

## Data safety answers

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
  - Audio files are processed on-device only and are not transmitted
    (unless the user configures their *own* server — data goes to a
    destination the user controls, which Play does not count as collection
    by the developer).
  - Lyrics lookup sends song title/artist (not personal data) to LRCLIB on
    explicit user action.
- Do you provide a way for users to request deletion? **Yes** — delete tracks
  in the app or uninstall.
