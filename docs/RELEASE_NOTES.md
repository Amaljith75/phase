## Phase 0.4

**Install:** `Phase-*-arm64-v8a.apk` for almost every phone (`armeabi-v7a` for
older 32-bit phones; `.aab` is for Google Play). Installs over the previous
version — your library, stems and mashups are kept.

### New
- **Simpler mashups** — each song gets its own four stem pads (vocals, drums,
  bass, other) just like the main player: drag up/down for level, double-tap
  to mute, swipe to solo. Under each song is its own waveform bar: drag the
  two handles to choose the part of the song that plays, tap to jump there,
  and move the song's start by bars.
- **Reset** on every performance tab (Modes, DJ, EDM) and **Stop** for pads.
- **Delete** mashups and saved mixes from Home, Library or the mashup screen.
- **Interface colour** — choose an accent colour and background in Settings.

### Changed
- Cleaner Settings: AI separation shows a single "Engine ready" light.
